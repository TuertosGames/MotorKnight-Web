THE VISION

01 / THE LIMBO

A fatal crash on the way to the hospital traps you in the Limbo del Asfalto. A dark dimension where your life hangs by a thread, and your only guide is a mysterious voice on the radio.

02 / THE LIGHT

Shadows lurk in the dark and are invulnerable to your attacks. Your survival depends on strategic positioning: use dynamic spotlights and your bike's headlights to weaken the darkness.

03 / ENGINE

Built from the ground up on our custom Runic2D Engine. Total control, absolute performance, zero compromises.

FEATURES

01 / DYNAMIC COMBAT

Engage in fast-paced top-down combat where positioning is everything. Stay in the light to damage the relentless 'Shadows', or perish in the darkness.

02 / ARSENAL & MAGIC

Wield a variety of weapons from Crossbows to Shotguns, and harness Light and Dark abilities like 'Solar Blessing' or 'Demonic Pillar' to survive.

03 / HIGH-SPEED RUNS

Travel between combat zones in side-scrolling high-speed chases. Dodge obstacles, repel projectiles, and collect scrap to upgrade your bike.
